What counts as a conversion

A verblijfsobject whose gebruiksdoel gains a woonfunctie; or a residential unit appearing in a pand built two or more years earlier that previously held only non-residential units; or residential units appearing after a Verbouwing pand status. Each VBO is counted once, at its first qualifying event. Totals are reconciled against CBS transformatie statistics per municipality and year.

How the forecast is made

One row per neighbourhood and year. Features: non-residential stock by use and build decade, floor area, last year's and the last three years' conversions here and in adjacent neighbourhoods, and lagged CBS indicators. Models: a seasonal-naive baseline, a negative-binomial GLM for interpretable coefficients, and a LightGBM model with a Tweedie objective and monotone constraints. Trained to 2022, validated on 2023, tested once on 2024.

No peeking

Every feature is computed from events the register had recorded by the forecast date, using tijdstipRegistratie rather than beginGeldigheid. A test recomputes the lag features from raw events and fails if the two disagree.

Boundaries

All history is re-aggregated by coordinate onto one boundary vintage (CBS Wijk- en buurtkaart, reference year in config.yaml), so municipal mergers and redrawn wijken do not break the panel. Only CBS covariates need a crosswalk, weighted by BAG address counts.
