How this was made

What is counted

A dwelling (verblijfsobject with woonfunctie) that appears in a building where office use disappeared, reconstructed from the full version history of the Kadaster BAG. Each dwelling is counted once, in the year its first residential version became valid. 2012–2024, whole country.

Small numbers

A neighbourhood-year with one or two conversions is shown hatched and reported as “fewer than 3”, following CBS practice for small cells. About 9% of all dwellings fall in such cells; they are included in national totals but not located on the map.

Boundaries

Every dwelling is placed by coordinate into the 2012 CBS Wijk- en buurtkaart, so later municipal mergers and redrawn neighbourhoods do not break the time series.

What is next

A one-year-ahead forecast of where the next conversions will happen, built on this detection plus non-residential stock, build decade and neighbouring activity. Code for both is in the repository.
